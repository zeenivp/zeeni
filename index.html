A little about me

I'm Zeenat — online I go by zeeni or zeenivp. I'm a beginner full stack developer falling deeper into the Nuxt and Cloudflare ecosystem one project at a time.

The edge computing model clicked for me in a big way. The idea that your app logic, database, storage, and cache all live as close to the user as possible — and that NuxtHub makes that surprisingly approachable — is what keeps me building.

Still learning. Always shipping. If something doesn't work, I enjoy figuring out why.
